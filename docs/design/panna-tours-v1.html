Every tip has an English line with a short Hindi line under it. The Hindi wording is a first draft for you to check. Tours never save words, add marks or change your review schedule themselves, and they return you to where you started.

How it looks

The tours (⋯ → Guided tours)

1 · Getting started offered on first visit

5 steps, about a minute. Starts on Today.

Tip (English, then Hindi)Points at
1Look up any word. Type an English word — or Hindi, in हिंदी or in English letters like chhodna.कोई भी शब्द खोजें — अंग्रेज़ी में, हिंदी में, या अंग्रेज़ी अक्षरों में।Search box
2Read like a real book. Desktop: turn pages with the arrows, by clicking a page corner, or with ← →. Phone: with the arrows, or swipe.तीरों से या उँगली से खिसकाकर पन्ने पलटें।The tour opens the book. Desktop: the page arrows · phone: the page arrows
3Tap a word for its card. See its Hindi meaning, hear it spoken, and find similar and opposite words.किसी शब्द को छुएँ — हिंदी अर्थ देखें और उच्चारण सुनें।The first word on the page
4Save words to learn them. Saved words come back in Review at the right times, so they stick.सहेजे गए शब्द सही समय पर Review में लौटते हैं, ताकि याद रहें।The tour opens that word’s card · the Save button (not pressed)
5A few minutes a day. Review quizzes you on your saved words, just when you’re about to forget them. More tours are in ⋯.Review आपके सहेजे शब्दों पर सवाल पूछता है। बाकी टूर ⋯ में हैं।Review (phone: bottom tab · desktop: header button). Then back to Today.

2 · The word card

5 steps. Opens a word that has similar and opposite words (today’s word of the day if it has some).

1Hindi first. The large red line is the Hindi meaning; the English definition is under it.बड़ी लाल पंक्ति हिंदी अर्थ है; नीचे अंग्रेज़ी परिभाषा।Hindi meaning
2Hear it. Listen to the English word, or to its Hindi meaning.अंग्रेज़ी शब्द या उसका हिंदी अर्थ सुनें।The two speak buttons
3Similar and opposite words. Tap one to open it right here — an easy way to grow your vocabulary.मिलते-जुलते और उल्टे शब्द — किसी को छूकर यहीं खोलें।Similar / Opposite chips
4Save to learn. Saving adds the word to Review. Tap again to remove it.सहेजने से शब्द Review में जुड़ता है। हटाने के लिए फिर छुएँ।Save
5Find it in the book. Shows the word on its page, underlined in pencil.शब्द को किताब में उसके पन्ने पर दिखाता है।“See on page”

3 · Saving and Review

4 steps. Opens Saved, then returns.

1Everything you keep. Saved has your words, the pages you marked, and your notes.Saved में आपके शब्द, निशान लगे पन्ने और नोट्स हैं।Saved (tab / header button); the tour opens it
2How well you know each word. Every saved word shows whether it’s new, being learned, or mastered.हर शब्द के साथ दिखता है: नया, सीख रहे हैं, या पक्का।Words · Pages · Notes switch
3How Review works. A word comes back after 1, 3, 7, 16 and 35 days. Right answers stretch the gap; a miss brings it back tomorrow.शब्द 1, 3, 7, 16 और 35 दिन बाद लौटता है। गलत जवाब पर कल फिर।Review (tab / header button)
4A little every day. No timer, no rush — keep your streak going. On a keyboard, answer with 1–4 and press Enter.कोई टाइमर नहीं — रोज़ थोड़ा दोहराएँ और अपनी streak बनाए रखें।Today’s review card. Desktop only: the keyboard sentence.

4 · Marking the book built after step 5

Desktop 4 steps: ribbon, folded corner, margin note, letter tabs. Phone 2 steps: ribbon, swipe. (Phones have no folded corners, notes or letter tabs yet; step 5 may change that.)

1Place a ribbon. Tap the ribbon to keep your place on this page.इस पन्ने पर रिबन लगाकर जगह याद रखें।Silk ribbon
2Fold a corner to mark a page you’ll come back to. (desktop)जिस पन्ने पर लौटना हो, उसका कोना मोड़ें।Page corner
3Write in the margin. Click the margin to leave a pencil note. (desktop)हाशिये पर क्लिक करके पेंसिल से नोट लिखें।Page margin
4Jump to a letter with the tabs on the edge of the book. (desktop)किनारे के अक्षरों से सीधे उस अक्षर पर जाएँ।Letter tabs

5 · Settings and backup

5 steps. Opens the ⋯ menu.

1Make it yours. Settings and more live here.सेटिंग्स और बाकी विकल्प यहाँ हैं।⋯ button; the tour opens the menu
2Evening light. A warmer, dimmer desk for reading at night.रात में पढ़ने के लिए हल्की, गर्म रोशनी।Desk lighting
3Page turns. Choose how pages turn; “Instant” skips the animation.पन्ने कैसे पलटें, चुनें; Instant में कोई एनिमेशन नहीं।Page-turn speed
4Keep your words safe. They live only in this browser. Back them up to a file, and restore it on any device.आपके शब्द सिर्फ़ इसी ब्राउज़र में हैं। फ़ाइल में सहेजें, किसी भी डिवाइस पर वापस लाएँ।Back up my words
5Tours any time. Replay any tour from here, as often as you like.कोई भी टूर यहाँ से जितनी बार चाहें देखें।Guided tours (new menu row)
